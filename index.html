Ten seconds a day

Pick a mood, write one sentence. That's a whole entry — which is exactly why you'll still be doing it next year.

What lifts you

Once you have a few weeks written down, Lumi counts them. It shows which of your own words and tags turn up on your better days — with the number of days behind each, so you can weigh it yourself.

Your year in pixels

Every logged day becomes a colored square. Tap any past day to read or quietly fix it. Once you have two years, see them side by side.

Ask your journal

Search everything you have ever written, or ask a plain question — "what did my best weeks have in common?" — and get an answer built only out of your own entries.

Never lose a word

Your line saves itself as you type. Close the app mid-thought — it's already there.

Reflections that remember

A short letter at the end of the week, longer ones at the end of every month and year — and each one knows what Lumi already told you. Your first reflection is free.

Your year as a little book

Turn a finished year into a small PDF booklet: every day in order, with the letters Lumi wrote you. Yours to keep or to print.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, nothing to log into. Your journal stays on your device — and so does the counting. Lock it behind Face ID if you want, and export it all to CSV whenever you like.

A nudge, not a nag

One optional daily reminder, at a time you choose. Miss a day and nothing breaks.

The journal itself is free, forever — unlimited entries, the full year grid, search, your insights, reminders and the app lock. Lumi Pro pays for the parts that read your days back to you: what lifts you, asking your journal, the letters, and the year booklet. Your first reflection and your first question are free, so you can read one about your own life before you decide anything.
