Privacy Policy

Lumi Journal · Last updated 22 July 2026

The short version. Lumi Journal has no accounts and collects no personal information. Your journal lives on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you personally tap to generate a weekly reflection — and even then, that week's entries are read once to write the reflection and are never stored on our servers. We don't track you, show ads, or sell data. Ever.

Who we are

Lumi Journal ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Firefly Software. Questions? Email fireflysoftware1@gmail.com.

No account, no sign-up

You don't create an account or give us your name, email, or phone number to use Lumi Journal. There is nothing to log into.

What stays on your device

Everything you write stays on your phone, in the app's local storage:

  • Your daily entries — the mood you pick and the single line you write, with the date.
  • Any weekly reflections you've generated.
  • Your settings (reminder on/off and time).

We cannot see this data. It never leaves your device except in the one case described next.

Weekly reflections (optional)

Lumi Journal can generate a short weekly reflection written by AI. This feature is optional and off until you ask for it. The first time you use it, we show a consent screen and ask you to agree.

When you tap to generate a reflection:

  • That week's entries (dates, moods, and lines) are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our reflection service, which forwards them to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the reflection text.
  • The reflection is returned to your device and saved there.
  • Your entries are processed only to create that reflection. They are not stored on our servers or by us. Our service keeps only a small counter (tied to a random ID, see below) so it can rate-limit requests, and that counter expires automatically.

Anthropic processes the request to generate the text and does not use it to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Our reflection service is hosted on Cloudflare.

Random install ID

To stop abuse of the reflection service, the app generates a random identifier (a UUID) the first time it runs and stores it on your device. It is sent only with reflection requests, purely so the service can count how many requests come from one install. It is not your name, email, or account — there is no account — and it is not linked to your identity or your entries.

Anonymous usage counts

So we can see, in aggregate, whether features are used, the app may send anonymous event counts (for example, "a reflection was generated"). These contain no identifier, no device information, and nothing that can be linked back to you — just a running tally.

Purchases

Weekly reflections are a paid subscription. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through our subscriptions provider, RevenueCat. We never see or receive your credit card or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data — there is nothing to sell.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data access.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your journal is stored locally, you are in control. You can export everything to a CSV file at any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes all local data from your device. Reflection requests leave nothing behind on our side beyond the temporary rate-limit counter, which expires on its own.

Children

Lumi Journal is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data a company holds about you. Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you on our servers, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve or erase — your data is on your device and under your control. If you have questions, contact us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for meaningful changes, note it in the app or its store listing.
